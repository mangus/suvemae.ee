# Vanaisa Puupakk

A run-away game. Vanaisa Puupakk (Grandpa Log) is an old man in a big wooden log costume. He hides behind the houses, jumps out from a corner and chases you, dancing, jumping and singing his own song.

## How to play

- Move with the arrow keys or WASD, or touch or click where you want to run.
- Stay away from the grandpa as long as you can. When he gets tired he jumps off to hide behind another house.
- Walk up into a house door to go inside: the rooms are a real 3D view (raycasting, like old first-person games). Turn and walk with the arrow keys or the buttons, and leave through the green VÄLJA door. Every house has its own rooms with windows, paintings, bookshelves, plants and lamps. Grandpa hides around a corner inside and jumps out when you come close (or when he gets tired of waiting). Pick up the stars: each one adds 3 seconds to your time. Grandpa looks like a real old man and limps on his stiff right leg. Hide under a bed and he cannot catch you: he limps back to his corner, but your time stops while you hide. Inside, the 3D view fills the whole screen: look around with the mouse (click to lock it, Esc lets go) or by dragging a finger on the view.
- He gets faster the longer you last. Your time can go on the high score table with a nickname.
